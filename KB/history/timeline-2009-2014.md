# Timeline: EVE Online 2009–2014 (Key Events)

This is a high-level timeline focusing on events, mechanics, and cultural moments relevant to the 2009–2014 period.

## 2009

- **Dominion** (December 2009)
  - Major sovereignty overhaul
  - Introduction of supercapitals as a real force
  - Infrastructure hubs and sovereignty structures
  - This expansion fundamentally changed how null-sec was played

## 2010

- **Tyrannis** (May 2010)
  - Planetary Interaction introduced
  - New source of passive income
  - Created a whole new layer of the industrial game

- **Incursion** (November 2010)
  - First major live event system
  - Sansha invasions across New Eden
  - Created high-end PvE content in high-sec

## 2011

- **Crucible** (November 2011)
  - One of the most beloved expansions by longtime players
  - Significant UI improvements
  - Major balance pass across many systems
  - Often cited as the point where EVE "felt the best" for many veterans
  - Many quality-of-life changes while still keeping the core dangerous feel

## 2012

- **Inferno** (May 2012)
  - Major missile rebalance
  - Crimewatch improvements
  - New destroyers and tactical destroyers coming into focus

- **Retribution** (December 2012)
  - Crimewatch 2.0
  - Major changes to how security status and criminal flagging worked
  - New ships and balance changes
  - This had a big impact on ganking and low-sec life

## 2013

- **Odyssey** (June 2013)
  - Big focus on exploration
  - Data and relic sites became much more rewarding
  - Scanning and hacking minigames updated

- **Rubicon** (November 2013)
  - Interceptors and new mobile structures
  - Mobile depots, cyno inhibitors, etc.
  - Small but meaningful changes to how people operated in space

- **Battle of Asakai** (January 2013)
  - One of the most famous fights in EVE history
  - A Titan was accidentally bridged into a system
  - Resulted in one of the largest battles ever at the time
  - Highlighted both the scale and the chaos possible in null-sec

## 2014

- **Kronos** (June 2014)
  - Marauder rebalance
  - Mission running received attention
  - Continued the trend of giving battleships more love

- **Crimson Harvest** and other events
  - Seasonal events starting to become more common

## Recurring Cultural Events

- **Hulkageddon** (multiple runs between 2010–2012)
  - Player-organized mining ship massacre events
  - See `player-stories/the-hulkageddon.md`

- **Burn Jita**
  - Goonswarm organized massive ganking operations in Jita
  - Became a recurring tradition

- **Incursion running communities**
  - Specialized groups formed around running Incursions for profit

## Broader Context

This period saw:
- The continued growth and consolidation of large null-sec coalitions
- The rise of "blue doughnut" politics in some regions
- The slow professionalization of some large alliances
- A player base that was still relatively "hardcore" compared to later years
- Mechanics that were often punishing but created strong memories
