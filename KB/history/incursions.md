# History: Incursions (2010–2014)

**Incursions** were introduced with the **Incursion** expansion in November 2010. They represented EVE Online's first major attempt at dynamic, server-wide live events.

## What Were Incursions?

Sansha's Nation would invade specific constellations. These invasions had several stages and created pockets of space with special rules and very strong NPC rats.

Key features:
- **Motherships** and **True Sansha** rats that were significantly stronger than normal
- **Vanguard, Assault, and Headquarters** sites
- A special **Incursion influence** system that affected the constellation
- **LP rewards** that were very lucrative at the time
- A sense that "something was happening in the universe"

## Impact on the Game (2010–2012)

### For High-Sec Players
- Suddenly there was high-end PvE content available in high-security space.
- Many mission runners and ratters moved into Incursion running.
- It created a new form of "endgame" PvE that wasn't just Level 4 missions.
- Fleets of 10–40 people would form to run the sites.

### For the Economy
- Incursions were a major ISK faucet during their peak.
- The LP rewards were extremely valuable.
- Certain modules and ships saw price spikes due to demand.

### For the Atmosphere
For the first time, there was content that felt like it was "happening to the universe" rather than just being instanced content. People would talk about which constellations were "incursed" in local and on forums.

## Evolution Over the Years

**2010–2011 (Launch)**
- Very new and exciting
- Balance was rough at first (some sites were extremely difficult)
- Many people made a lot of money

**2012–2013**
- Incursions became a stable part of the high-sec economy
- "Incursion runners" became a recognized playstyle
- Some groups specialized in them almost exclusively

**2014**
- Still popular, but the novelty had worn off for many
- CCP had made various adjustments to rewards and difficulty over time

## Famous (and Infamous) Aspects

- **Mothership sites** were the big money makers but required significant fleets.
- There were occasional horror stories of people losing expensive ships to the rats.
- Some players loved the content. Others hated that it "ruined" certain high-sec areas temporarily.
- Incursions created interesting interactions when they appeared in low-sec or near null-sec borders.

## Cultural Legacy

Incursions are remembered as one of the first times CCP tried to make the universe feel *alive* in a meaningful way. While later events (like the Drifters or the Triglavian invasion) built on this idea, Incursions were the first real taste of it for many players.

For many people who played during 2011–2013, running Incursions is one of their strongest memories of "what high-sec endgame looked like."

**Notes for EvEmu**:
- Incursions are a major feature that helps make the universe feel dynamic
- The LP rewards and site difficulty need to be tuned correctly for the era
- The social aspect (forming fleets, sharing sites) is important
- The "constellation is under incursion" feeling should be communicated to players
