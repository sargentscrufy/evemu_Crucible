# Ichoria and Samanuni Systems (Crucible Era 2009-2014)

These systems in Black Rise region were notable mission hubs for Caldari Navy agents during the era, especially for high-sec mission running near Jita area. Black Rise was added for Faction Warfare, making these systems relevant for Caldari space activities.

## Samanuni

- **Region**: Black Rise
- **Constellation**: Aokinen
- **Security**: 0.7
- **Faction**: Caldari State
- **Local Pirates**: Guristas
- **Notable Stations**:
  - Samanuni 11 - Caldari Navy Warehouse (Caldari Navy)
  - Samanuni 6 - Caldari Navy Anchorage (Caldari Navy)
  - Samanuni 3 - Wiyrkomi Corporation Factory (Wiyrkomi Corporation)
  - Samanuni 8 - Caldari Constructions Logistic Support (Caldari Constructions)

## Ichoriya (Ichoriya / Ichoria)

- **Region**: Black Rise (pre-Pochven; system was part of empire space taken into Pochven in 2020)
- **Constellation**: Likely Aokinen or adjacent in Black Rise area
- **Security**: 0.6
- **Faction**: Caldari State
- **Notable**: Good mission hub with high quality Caldari Navy agents in the same station. Popular for L4 missions.

### Agents in Ichoriya (era data)

- At least 2 Level 4 Caldari Navy Security agents in the same station (notable for efficient mission running without traveling between stations).
- The system was recommended in 2012 player discussions as a strong Caldari Navy mission area with good sec status.

(Exact full list for the era is sparse in public archives; focused on CN L4 for security missions. Other divisions may have been present in the system or nearby.)
