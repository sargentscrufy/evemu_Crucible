# EVE Online Knowledge Base (2009–2014)

This folder is a living archive of the **EVE Online Crucible era** — roughly 2009 through 2014.

The goal is to preserve the *feel*, mechanics, culture, and stories of this period, especially for projects like EvEmu that aim to recreate the experience of that time.

## Purpose

- Record accurate historical context of EVE between 2009 and 2014
- Document classic **gameplay loops** as they actually existed
- Capture **player stories**, anecdotes, and the culture of the time
- Serve as reference material for faithful simulation in private servers

## Folder Structure

```
KB/

├── player-stories/         # First-person style accounts and famous tales

```

## Time Period Focus

This KB concentrates on the years **2009–2014**, with particular emphasis on the **Crucible expansion (2011)** and surrounding years. This was a very distinct era in EVE's history:

- Pre-UI modernization in many ways (Crucible brought big changes)
- Different sovereignty and capital gameplay
- Classic mission running, ratting, and mining loops
- The rise of large nullsec coalitions
- Famous live events (Incursions)
- Old crimewatch and security status systems
- Different industry and market dynamics

## Contributing

Feel free to add new files or expand existing ones. When writing:

- Be specific about dates and expansions when possible
- Describe mechanics as they actually worked at the time (not modern EVE)
- Include flavor and player perspective where appropriate
- Cite sources or note when something is anecdotal
