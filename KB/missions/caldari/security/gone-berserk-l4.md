# Gone Berserk, Level 4

**Agent / Corp / Location**: Varies (EoM or Guristas in Caldari space reports).

**Briefing / Story**: "We have reports of terrorists wreaking havoc in <system>. Our convoys have been attacked, as well as some outlying outposts; they seem to be intent on causing as much damage as possible. I'd like you to take care of them as quickly as possible. And be aware that they may not all be gathered together when you arrive... if so, you will be able to pick them off as they warp in to the rendezvous point."

**Objectives**: Destroy all hostile ships (EoM Death Lord from all chains).

**Encounters** (Ungated single pocket; 3 initial groups with Cruiser and Battleship in each; each Cruiser killed triggers reinforcement wave; 3 chains total; from era Uni fetch):

## Initial Defenders
- 3x Cruiser (EoM Crusader) **Each one triggers reinforcement wave 1 on death**
- 3x Battleship (EoM Hydra)

**Initial Defenders**
- 3x Cruiser (EoM Crusader) **Each one triggers reinforcement wave 1 on death**
- 3x Battleship (EoM Hydra)

**Reinforcement Wave 1 (x3)**
- 2x Cruiser (EoM Death Knight) **Last one triggers reinforcement wave 2 on death**

**Reinforcement Wave 2 (x3)**
- 0-2x Frigate (EoM Saboteur)
- 1x Battleship (EoM Hydra) **Triggers reinforcement wave 3 on death**

**Reinforcement Wave 3 (x3)**
- 1x Battleship (EoM Hydra)
- 1x Battleship (EoM Ogre) **Triggers reinforcement wave 4 on death**

**Reinforcement Wave 4 (x3)**
- 1x Battleship (EoM Death Lord)

**Spawn Triggers**: Kill specific Cruiser (last in chain) spawns next wave of 4. All chains must be cleared.

**Rewards / LP / Bounty notes** (era): Bounties: 10.77M ISK; Loot & Salvage: 14.4M ISK.

**Strategy / Approach Loops**: Manage aggro per chain; work on single chain of 4 waves/spawns, then kill next Crusader. Flag Cruisers. Passive drones to avoid triggering swarm. AI different; scooping/releasing drones may not shift aggro.

**Era Notes / Caldari Flavor**: Terrorists (EoM in some reports for Caldari space); multi-wave spawn chains.
