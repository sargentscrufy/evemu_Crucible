**Briefing / Story**: Locate and recover the hidden stash / objective from warehouse in pirate-held deadspace. Corporate recovery theme vs Guristas.

**Objectives**: Clear or manage aggro, access warehouse for mission objective item, return.

**Encounters** (Single deadspace pocket; all aggro on warp-in; small frig group + structures with priority target).

**Era Notes / Caldari Flavor**: Classic L1 "find the stash" vs Guristas. Mining overlap possible (Veldspar asteroids noted in era comments for Guristas variant). Low risk intro.
