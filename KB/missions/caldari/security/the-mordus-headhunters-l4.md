# The Mordus Headhunters, Level 4

**Agent / Corp / Location**: Mordu's Legion agents (often offered in Caldari space via security agents or specific; high-sec). Deadspace pockets.

**Briefing / Story**: Mordu's Legion headhunters / bounty operations targeting threats. Two-pocket deadspace encounter with multiple spread groups; careful aggro management key (Mordus use Kin/EM, webs on ints).

**Objectives**: Clear pockets (blitz possible by selective group kill in pocket 2).

**Encounters** (2 pockets, multiple groups, auto aggro on attack in some; web/scram on Bounty Hunter ints; no accel gates noted in primary report; positioning critical to avoid full room aggro. Sentry drones risky for aggro).

### 1st Pocket
#### 1st Group (12-30km, Beacon) - Auto aggro. Attack draws group 2 aggro.
- 5x Cruisers (Mordus Leopard/Lion)
- 3x Battleship (Mordus Phanti/Gigamar/Sequestor)

#### 2nd Group (65-90km, Minmatar Mining Station)
- 4x Frigate (Mordus Gladius)
- 2x Interceptor (Bounty Hunter)
- 5x Cruiser (Mordus Puma/Lion)

#### 3rd Group (76-90km, Minmatar Trade Station Ruins)
- 6x Frigate (Mordus Katana/Gladius)
- 4x Cruiser (Mordus Bobcat)
- 2x Battleship (Mordus Sequestor)

### 2nd Pocket
#### 1st Group (16-21km, Veldspar Asteroid)
- 8x Frigate (Mordus Sabre/Katana)
- 2x Interceptor (Mordus Bounty Hunter) **Webifier/Warp Disruptor**

#### 2nd Group (39-74km, Ghost Ship)
- 5x Frigate (Mordus Gladius)
- 12x Cruiser (Mordus Bobcat/Cheetah/Leopard/Lion/Puma)
- 5x Battleship (Mordus Sequestor/Phanti/Gigamar)

#### 3rd Group (75-100km, Gallente Station Ruins, Right)
- 4x Frigate (Mordus Katana)
- 5x Cruiser (Mordus Leopard/Lion)
- 3x Battleship (Mordus Sequestor/Mammoth)

#### 4th Group (106-121km, Gallente Station Ruins, Left)
- 7x Frigate (Mordus Katana/Mordus Bounty Hunter)
- 8x Cruiser (Mordus Cheetah/Mordus Katana)

**Blitz**:
- 1st Pocket: Kill all ships.
- 2nd Pocket: Kill only the 3rd group (includes Mordus Mammoth BS). Position at right edge of group to aggro only that group, avoid full room.

**Strategy / Approach Loops**: High aggro awareness. Use positioning (right edge, transversal for lasers) to isolate groups. Prioritize webs/scrams (Bounty Hunters). Avoid broad drone aggro. Recommended ships era: Raven, Dominix, Ishtar, Paladin, etc. Damage Kin/EM. Close range ammo notes for proj.

**Mining**: Asteroids in pockets (Veld, Pyrox, Plag).

**Rewards / Era notes**: Bounty ~10.6M, loot/salvage ~17M (era estimates). Hard due to spread + potential full aggro.
