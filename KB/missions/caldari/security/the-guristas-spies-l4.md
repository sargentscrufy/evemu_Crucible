# The Guristas Spies, Level 4 (Guristas)

**Agent / Corp / Location**: Spacelane Patrol / Caldari Navy agents (high-sec Caldari, e.g. reports name Lozdod Pousel, Osmon 0.7). Deadspace single pocket.

**Briefing / Story**: Guristas agents have infiltrated an outpost and are planning something big. Eliminate the Guristas Spies and their guards. Groups spawn in short intervals after arrival.

**Objectives**: Kill the group containing the Guristas Spies (the Battlecruisers marked Guristas Spy) to complete. Optional full clear for bounties.

**Encounters** (Single deadspace pocket; groups spawn quickly in intervals; possible initial agro or not; drones ignored by non-aggroed groups; jamming possible. Spawns may vary slightly.)

#### Group 1 (23 - 30km)
- 1x Destroyer (Pithior Supremacist/Terrorist)
- 3x Battleships (Pith Extinguisher/Destroyer/Exterminator)

#### Group 2 (30km)
- 3x Battlecruisers (Pithatis Assaulter/Enforcer)
- 4x Battleships (Pith Eradicator/Obliterater/Exterminator/Extinguisher)

#### Group 3 (57 - 80km)
- 2x Destroyers (Pithior Supremacist/Terrorist)
- 3x Battlecruisers (Guristas Spy)
- 1x Battleship (Pith Massacrer/Conquistador)

**Blitz**: Microjump or burn past non-spy groups and focus the Guristas Spy group (3x BC Guristas Spy). Mission completes on killing the spy group. Attacking spies may draw aggro from others.

**Strategy / Approach Loops**: Long-range BS or drone boat for easy clear or blitz. Kill spy group for completion; full room for max bounty/loot. Watch for jamming. Recommended Kin/Therm. Example bounties ~6.8M, loot ~2.5M (era).

**Era Notes / Caldari Flavor**: Classic L4 anti-infiltration vs Guristas for Caldari Navy/Spacelane agents. Easy afk option noted in reports. Part of short chains to corporate data missions.
