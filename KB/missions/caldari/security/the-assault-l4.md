# The Assault, Level 4 (Guristas)

**Agent / Corp / Location**: Caldari Navy/Spacelane Patrol agents (high-sec Caldari).

**Briefing / Story**: Assault on Guristas positions (2 pockets).

**Objectives**: Clear pockets (blitz group 3 in pocket 2).

**Encounters** (Deadspace; auto aggro; web/scram on elite frig; jam; triggers highest bounty BS; from era reports):

## Pocket 1
- 1st Group (15-31km auto): 2x Elite Frigates (Dire Pithi Wrecker/Plunderer) **Web/Scram**, 4x Battleships (Pith Dismantler/Obliterator/Destroyer/Eliminator) **Trigger, Jam**
- 2nd Group (75-85km): 4x Elite Cruisers (Dire Pithum Inferno/Mortifier) **Jam**, 3x Battlecruisers (Pithatis Assassin/Assaulter)
- Reinforcement (117-120km auto): 3-4x Battlecruisers (Pithatis Assassin/Enforcer/Executor/Revolter), 3-4x Battleships (Pith Conquistador/Eliminator/Exterminator/Destroyer) **Jam**

## Pocket 2 (aggro notes)
- 1st Group (50-60km): 2x Elite Frigates (Dire Pithi Invader/Infiltrator) **Web/Scram**, 2x Elite Cruisers (Dire Pithum Abolisher/Eraser), 3x Battleships (Pith Dismantler/Extinguisher)
- 2nd Group (65-70km): 2x Elite Cruisers (Dire Pithum Annihilator/Killer/Murderer/Nullifier) **Jam**, 5x Battleships (Pith Extinguisher/Destroyer/Eliminator/Conquistador) **Trigger, Jam**
- 3rd Group (110km): 2x Elite Frigates (Dire Pithi Infiltrator/Invader) **Web/Scram**, 3x Destroyers (Pithior Guerilla/Renegade), 3x Battlecruisers (Pithatis Assaulter/Enforcer), 2x Battleships (Pith Exterminator/Extinguisher/Destroyer/Conquistador) **Jam**
- 1st Reinforcement (40km auto last ship): 4x Cruisers (Pithum Eraser/Inferno/Mortifier/Abolisher), 4x Battleships (Pith Eliminator/Exterminator/Conquistador) **Jam**
- 2nd Reinforcement: 2x Cruisers (Pithum Eraser/Inferno/Mortifier)

**Blitz**: Pocket 1 warp through gate without killing anything. Pocket 2: eliminate all ships in Group 3. Warp out. Destroy Starbase Control Tower prevents reinforcement (but aggro group 2).

**Mining**: Pocket 1: 60 Veldspar asteroids (~2.4M units).

**Rewards / LP / Bounty notes** (era): Bounty ~20 mil, Loot + Salvage ~6.2 mil.

**Strategy / Approach Loops**: Kill web/scram first. Destroy structures for blitz where noted. Manage aggro (fleet notes in reports).

**Era Notes / Caldari Flavor**: Guristas positions; structures; web/jam heavy. Common for Caldari security agents.
