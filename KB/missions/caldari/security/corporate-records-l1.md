# Corporate Records, Level 1 (Guristas)

**Agent / Corp / Location**: Guristas-related security agents or Spacelane Patrol linked (high-sec Caldari intro).

**Briefing / Story**: Corporate records / data theft context vs Guristas. Recover objective from cargo container in deadspace after clearing or bypassing guards.

**Objectives**: Reach cargo container (objective item) in pocket; loot and return. Optional full clear.

**Encounters** (Deadspace with gate; one main auto-aggro group of frigs; possible additional in pocket after gate).

#### Pocket 1
Gate 28km from Cargo Container objective.

**Group 1 (20km) Auto Aggro**:
- 7x Frigate (Pithi Invader, Pithi Infiltrator, Pithi Arrogator)

**Blitz**: After gate, fly directly to and loot the cargo container, leave without engaging additional (5x frigs + 2x dests possible in further area). AB highly recommended for speed.

**Mineable Asteroids**: 9x Veldspar, 9x Scordite, 9x Pyroxeres.

**Structures**: None notable.

**Rewards**: Basic L1, possible small loot from frigs if cleared.

**Strategy / Approach Loops**: Frigate with AB for blitz. Warp in, gate, speed to can, loot, exit. Or clear the 7 frigs for safety/loot. Simple.

**Era Notes / Caldari Flavor**: L1 corporate data recovery vs Guristas. Often part of early security or chain intros.
