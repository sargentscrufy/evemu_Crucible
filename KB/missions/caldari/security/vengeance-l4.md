# Vengeance, Level 4 (Guristas)

**Agent / Corp / Location**: Caldari Navy/Spacelane Patrol agents (high-sec Caldari).

**Briefing / Story**: Vengeance theme vs Guristas (revenge for attacks; full in reports).

**Objectives**: Clear pockets (must clear first 2 to proceed to 3rd).

**Encounters** (Deadspace; 3 pockets; drones may aggro; web/scram on frigs; jamming on some BS; named Rachen Mysuna in pocket 3 with shield boost and defender; from verbatim fetch):
