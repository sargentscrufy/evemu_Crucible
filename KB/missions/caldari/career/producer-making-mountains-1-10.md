# Producer - Making Mountains of Molehills (1-10) - Full Intro Mining / Hauling / Industry Career Arc (2009-2014)

**Agent / Corp / Location**: Industrialist - Producer career agent. Caldari examples: Science and Trade Institute (STI) or State and Region Bank (SAK) stations e.g. Jouvulen, Akiainavas, Uitra. High-sec Caldari space (0.5-1.0). Agent level 1 intro.

**Briefing / Story**: Corporate Caldari industrialist themes: "war never stops, and we need to stoke it with raw materials." Progressive training in mining, reprocessing, manufacturing, hauling, basic combat for industry protection. Player proves self as industrial mogul. Ends with building a frigate and transition advice to mining agents.

**Objectives / Exact Steps** (verbatim from era guides ~2011-2014 wckg.net record; doable in corvette initially, Venture reward for mining path. Quantities approximate, exact turn-in per mission journal):

1. **Making Mountains of Molehills (1 of 10)**: Mine and deliver 1000 units Veldspar (or "at least some ore") from uploaded bookmark location (typically 1 jump, asteroid belt). Fit mining module (Civilian Miner). Multiple trips OK.  
   **Reward**: Miner I, ISK.

2. **Making Mountains of Molehills (2 of 10)**: Reprocess at least 100 Veldspar (or sufficient for 100 Tritanium) at station Reprocessing Plant. Deliver Tritanium.  
   **Reward**: Venture (mining frigate), ISK.

3. **Making Mountains of Molehills (3 of 10)**: Acquire and deliver 1 x 1MN Civilian Afterburner (packaged/"new in box"). Can build from BP or buy or use repackage trick on corvette fittings.  
   **Reward**: Expanded Cargohold I, ISK.

4. **Making Mountains of Molehills (4 of 10)**: Warp to drone-infested belt (Rogue Drones). Kill hostiles (drones/NPC), mine ~3500 Veldspar (refine to ~7000 Tritanium) or buy equiv Tritanium (~30k ISK). Deliver Tritanium.  
   **Reward**: Mining Laser Upgrade I, Miner I, ISK.  
   **Notes**: Combat + mine combo. Can use drones/hobgoblins for kill.

5. **Making Mountains of Molehills (5 of 10)**: Courier/hauling: Transport electronic parts (~40m3) to remote station (e.g. Abagawa IV - Moon 2 - Hyasyoda Corporation Mineral Reserve, ~7 jumps, varies by agent). Return to agent.  
   **Reward**: Expanded Cargohold I, 1MN Afterburner I, ISK.

6. **Making Mountains of Molehills (6 of 10)**: Manufacture 20 x Cap Booster 25. Source minerals (Tritanium, Pyerite, Mexallon) via mining Scordite/Plagioclase or buy. Run build job (~9 min). Deliver.  
   **Reward**: ISK.  
   **Notes**: Or shortcut buy on market cheaply.

7. **Making Mountains of Molehills (7 of 10)**: Courier: Pick up Cap Boosters from hangar, deliver 1 jump to buyer station (e.g. Unpas VIII - Moon 11 - Prompt Delivery Storage, varies).  
   **Reward**: Damage Control I, ISK.

8. **Making Mountains of Molehills (8 of 10)**: Manufacture a Shuttle using provided BPC (or sufficient runs). Source ~1200 Veldspar / 2640 Tritanium (buy or mine). Deliver built shuttle.  
   **Reward**: ISK.

9. **Making Mountains of Molehills (9 of 10)**: Mining decoy op: Warp to site, fit/use mining laser to draw out enemy (spy/pirate NPC shows while mining). Kill NPC, loot "Production Assistant" from wreck/site. May keep ore (often Kernite present).  
   **Reward**: ISK.  
   **Notes**: Combat trigger on mining. Good kernite for AIR program too.

10. **Making Mountains of Molehills (10 of 10)**: Build a Bantam (or equivalent frigate) using provided BPC. Gather mats (approx 22,800 Tritanium, 4,275 Pyerite, 1,782 Mexallon, 357 Isogen; e.g. refine kernite/pyroxeres/veldspar). Deliver the ship.  
    **Reward**: Hauler (Bantam or similar), ISK.  
    **Debrief**: "Congratulations... you've proven that you have what it takes." Advice to seek mining agents or other careers.

**Rewards / LP / Bounty notes (era)**: Progressive modules (miners, cargo expanders, AB, DCU, MLU), ships (Venture, Bantam/hauler), ISK, skill familiarity. No significant LP at L1 career. Standings to corp/agent.

**Strategy / Approach Loops**: 
- Early: Corvette (repackage for free fittings/miners/guns). Mine in belts (right-click space > Agent missions bookmark).
- Mid: Use Venture for mining efficiency. Industry window for builds. Market buy shortcuts for cheap items.
- Courier: Use cargo, avoid AP if possible for speed; set destination from agent.
- Combat: Simple small NPC spawns; civilian guns or drones. Kill before/while mining.
- Blitz/optimize: Buy low-value items on market instead of mining/build for time. Stack items properly. Use AIR Career Program overlap for mining quotas.
- Final: Accumulate ore from prior or buy; build in station.

**Locations**: Agent-specific bookmarks (1-9 jumps typical for Caldari highsec). Belts, stations (e.g. Hyasyoda, Prompt Delivery). Deadspace not typical; simple belts + station turns.

**Era Notes / Caldari Flavor**: Intro for new players to industry/mining/hauling loop in Caldari corporate efficiency culture. "War never stops" militarized industry theme. Rewards teach basic fitting, industry jobs, reprocessing, courier. Matches 2009-2014 career agent structure (pre-AIR heavy but similar). Often run alongside Entrepreneur arc.
