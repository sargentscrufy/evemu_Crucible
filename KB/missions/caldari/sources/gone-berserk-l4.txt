<!-- Header ID generation took 0.000023 seconds --><h1 id="hn_Gone_Berserk_Level_4"><a class="heading" href="https://eve-survival.org/?wakka=GoneBerserk4#">Gone Berserk, Level 4</a></h1>

<br />
<div class="lastedit">Last edited by <a class="user" href="https://eve-survival.org/?wakka=JeanInkura" title="Open user profile for JeanInkura">JeanInkura</a>:<br/><span class="lastedit_notes">Replaces old-style internal links with new pipe-split links.</span><br /> Mon, 16 Oct 2017 08:49 UTC</div><br />
<br />
Faction: EoM<br />
Mission type: Encounter<br />
Space type: Normal (MWD works)<br />
Damage dealt: Kinetic (68.76%)/ Thermal (31.24%)<br />
Web/ Scramble: EoM Saboteur  (Extra spawn, from the left group wave)<br />
Recommended damage dealing: Kinetic/ Explosive<br />
Recommended ship classes: Battleship, T2 or T3 Cruiser<br />
Video: <a class="ext" href="http://youtu.be/b30LVm-AHFg">Ishtar</a>, <a class="ext" href="https://youtu.be/ZEO-tOdZgCY">Raven Navy Issue</a><br />
<br />
<br />
Auto aggro from Initial Group and spawns.<br />
May [Fleet] warp to preferred distance. <br />
The initial formation is composed of three groups with a Cruiser and a Battleship in each.<br />
<ul>
	<li> Each Cruiser killed will trigger a spawn wave.</li>
</ul>

<ul>
	<li> Each wave is composed of four spawns for a total of 12 spawns.</li>
</ul>

<strong>Reminder</strong>: set your drones to passive to avoid triggering a swarm.<br />
Spawn composition and distance may vary with each wave.<br />

<h5 id="hn_Tip"><a class="heading" href="https://eve-survival.org/?wakka=GoneBerserk4#">Tip</a></h5>

To manage aggro, work on a single wave, then kill the next <em>Crusader</em> Cruiser.<br />
Flag the Cruisers at the beginning to make it easier to keep an eye on the triggers.<br />
The mission is flagged completed once you have killed the last <em>EoM Death Lord</em>.<br />
<br />
<h5 id="hn_Mining"><a class="heading" href="https://eve-survival.org/?wakka=GoneBerserk4#">Mining</a></h5>
